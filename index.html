What we stand for

We believe no animal should suffer on the streets. Our work has three simple steps.

Rescue

We pick up injured, sick and abandoned cats and dogs and bring them to safety.

Rehabilitate

Vet care, vaccines, good food and lots of love until they are healthy and happy.

Rehome

We carefully match each pet with a kind family through adoption and fostering.
