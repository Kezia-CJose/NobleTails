For Your Information

Approximate daily dry-food requirements:

Adult Cats

40–65 grams per day for an average 3–5 kg cat.

Kittens

30–55 grams (2–4 months) and 55–75 grams (4–6 months). Older kittens need age-appropriate portions.

Adult Dogs

Approx. 110 g for a 5 kg dog, 175 g for a 10 kg dog and 325 g for a 25 kg dog.

Puppies

Food requirements depend on age, breed and expected adult weight. Growing puppies need specially formulated puppy food.

Note: These are reference estimates, not fixed portions. Always check the food packaging and consult a veterinarian for individual feeding requirements.
