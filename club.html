NobleTails Volunteer Club

A student club for people who love animals and want to do something for them.

Join the club

What the club does

Feeding drives

Weekly food runs for street dogs and cats around campus.

Foster network

Members give temporary homes to pets recovering from injury.

Adoption camps

We organise camps where families can meet pets ready for rehoming.

Awareness

Posters, talks and social media about vaccination and adoption.

Roles you can take

  • Rescue team - go out and help pick up animals in need
  • Foster parent - look after a pet at home for a few weeks
  • Medical helper - assist with vet visits and medicines
  • Social media - make posts and photos of pets
  • Fundraising - run donation drives

Club meetings
